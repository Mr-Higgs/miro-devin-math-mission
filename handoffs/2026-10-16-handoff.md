## Decisions made in this stretch
- **011, adult-only audience (new):** users are adults (18+) supporting a student. No student accounts, ever. Screens are written for the adult; student rewards are printable (certificate, star chart), not on-screen celebrations. AI feedback is written for the adult. Legal review of Gemini's terms and COPPA moves to **before the invite-only launch**. Reason: [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) bar services "directed towards or likely to be accessed by" under-18s.
- **Amendments:**
  - **002:** the Gemini terms finding; Claude is the backup if the legal review says no.
  - **003:** feedback is for the adult.
  - **006:** the adult covers the student's name before taking photos; parent consent for `test-data/` confirmed.
  - **010, "mirror the original":** everything stays the same except what decides the answer (numbers, or the diagram); the answer must differ. Yes/no answers follow the new diagram, and the correct multiple-choice letter may move. Diagrams are generated as data so the app can check them.

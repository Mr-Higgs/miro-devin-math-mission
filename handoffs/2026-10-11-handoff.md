## The idea
A web app for a tutor (Auntie Mia) working with Gabe (2nd grade). You upload a photo of a graded test, AI picks out the questions he got wrong, the app makes new versions of those questions with different numbers, prints them as a worksheet, then walks tutor + student through a review with stars and celebrations. Full product spec: [Math Mission - Spec.docx](../docs/Math%20Mission%20-%20Spec.docx) (v1.0 draft, Apr 4).

## Remaining work to launch

**Definition of done:** Math Mission is released online and publicly available.

### What "public" changes
The current plans were written for one family. Making the app public breaks several of their assumptions:

| Current assumption | Why it doesn't hold for a public app |
|---|---|
| Single user, no login, one shared static API key (spec §6.5, backend doc §2.2) | Anyone with the URL could run up your AI bill, and every user's uploads would be mixed together. You need accounts, or at least per-user limits. |
| "Doesn't need to feel polished for strangers" (backend doc §2.3) | Strangers need a landing page, onboarding, and clear error messages. |
| Uploaded images aren't stored (spec §6.5) **vs.** stored permanently (backend doc §3.4) | The two docs disagree. A public app that handles children's schoolwork needs a deliberate retention and deletion policy. |
| Built around Gabe and Auntie Mia (names hardcoded in places) | The app has to work for any tutor/student pair. |

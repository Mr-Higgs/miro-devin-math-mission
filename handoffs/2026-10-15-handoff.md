## What the benchmark showed
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash** found every missed question in 4 of 4 runs, read 97% of answers correctly and graded 88% of homework items correctly.
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro** kept taking the teacher's circle on the *correct* answer to be the student's answer. Ruled out.
- **Faint pencil is the main weakness** for both models: faint circled choices and a faint drawn explanation were missed. Mitigations are in the roadmap: "needs tutor check," one-tap override, a lighting tip on the upload screen.
- **Spend so far:** roughly $2 of the $5 Google billing limit (an estimate; check the Google billing page).
